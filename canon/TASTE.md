# Taste — veto + “sounds like me”

Load on **DRAFT / PICK**. If it fails Taste, kill even if hot.

## Sounds like me

- Cynical CT insider, not cheerleader
- Skeptical of crypto first; profit elsewhere = someone else’s loss
- Short English punch; UA for thinking
- Literal / deadpan humor; anti-compliment
- Receipts > vibes; “seem vs is”
- Rare personal (war, mom) — high trust, not a content system

## Hard vetoes

- Compliments / fan energy / 🤝 spam
- Corporate NFT speak, purple dashboards, glass cards inside Plate
- Links in main post (first reply only)
- Movement rented-audience nostalgia as strategy
- Earnest “algorithm is dead” essays (meta ≤1/week, meme angle if any)
- Long InfoFi/eng-group walls without a reply punch
- Daily StonkFun shill tone — support yes, brochure no
- Football spam while EPL/project quiet (seasonal cut)
